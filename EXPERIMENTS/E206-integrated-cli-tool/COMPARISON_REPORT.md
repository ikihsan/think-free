## Executive Summary

This report presents the comprehensive assessment of all 5 IT/software PSC codes using the E206 integrated CLI tool. **All screen metrics reproduce E199/E200 baselines within 0.1 percentage points.**

## Detailed Findings by PSC Code

### 1. 7030 — BEST OVERALL for Small Software Teams ✅ PROCEED

**Strengths:**
- Highest screen score (2,411) — best balance of recipient count (3,916) and low concentration (38.4% top-10)
- Strong software market: 64.5% of awards, 75.6% of obligation
- Good small-team entry: 32.1% ≤ $40k, 46.8% ≤ $100k, 100% single-award
- Broadest civilian agency base (14+ agencies > $500M)
- Large market: $25.83B total obligation, $4.74B in sampled awards

**Weaknesses:**
- Small vendors sell direct (88.7% Direct/Unknown) — minimal GWAC usage (4.7%)
- GSA MAS small vendor share only 5-6% in software SINs despite 85% affinity
- SEWP Category B small vendor share only 6%
- Dominated by large resellers (Carahsoft, DLT, Dell, CDW, Four LLC)

**Entry Strategy:**
- **Primary**: Direct sales to 3,916 recipients (32% ≤ $40k)
- **Secondary**: Subcontract to Carahsoft, DLT, CDW, Four LLC
- **GWAC**: Low yield on GSA MAS (5.9%) and SEWP (6.0%)

---

### 2. DA10 — STRONG ALTERNATIVE with Unique GSA Schedule Channel ✅ PROCEED

**Strengths:**
- **Only code with meaningful GWAC usage by small vendors: 28.2% via GSA MAS + SEWP**
- GSA Schedule channel confirmed: 7.8% awarding agency, 19.4% VA funding
- Best software award accessibility: 49.9% of software ≤ $40k, median $40,168
- High software %: 57.8% of awards, 64.7% of obligation
- Strong small vendor GSA MAS share: 28% in 511210/511210S/518210C ($1.5B+ market)
- Subcontracting pathway exists: 8.1% sub/prime ratio, 85 unique subawardees

**Weaknesses:**
- Lower ≤ $40k fraction (27.1%) vs 7030 (32.1%)
- Higher top-10 concentration in full period (59.1% vs 45.3% in 7030)
- DOD-dominated (43.6%) but VA provides significant civilian alternative

**Entry Strategy:**
- **Primary**: GSA MAS Schedule 70 — 511210 (Software), 511210S (SaaS), 518210C (Cloud)
- **Secondary**: Subcontract to large primes (Peraton, Highlight, Carahsoft, Accenture)
- **Tertiary**: Direct sales (41.8% of small vendor $)
- **VA Channel**: Franchise Fund, Medical Care — $4.5B+ in federal accounts

---

### 3. 7A21 — HIGH CONCENTRATION, Carahsoft-Dominated ⚠️ CAUTION

**Strengths:**
- Highest software %: 71.8% of awards, 84.1% of obligation
- Good small-team entry: 31.1% ≤ $40k, 48.0% ≤ $100k
- Large market: $29.22B total obligation

**Weaknesses:**
- **Extreme concentration: Carahsoft 27.1% of sampled obligation**
- Top-10 share 48.8% (recipient) → 62.3% (award sample)
- Small vendors use Direct/Unknown (80.7%), minimal GWAC (8.6%)
- GSA MAS small vendor share only 3-4% in software SINs
- Army/Navy dominated (ITES 16.5%, SEAPORT 12.5%)
- New entrant share declining: 21.7% → 13.0% (E204)

**Entry Strategy:**
- **Avoid prime GWAC positions** — dominated by Carahsoft/Dell/Leidos
- **Subcontract** to Carahsoft, Dell, WWTech, Emergent
- **Direct sales** to niche requirements (but high concentration limits opportunity)

---

### 4. DA01 — LARGEST MARKET but Services-Dominated ⚠️ CAUTION

**Strengths:**
- Largest total market: $94.94B obligation
- Lowest top-10 recipient concentration: 34.5%
- High GSA awarding agency share: 13.6% (Acquisition Services Fund)
- Large civilian base: VA 16.6%, HHS 12.6%

**Weaknesses:**
- **Only 12.8% software awards** — services/integration market (78.4% services)
- Software median award: $383,186 (too large for small teams)
- Poor small-team software entry: 15.6% ≤ $40k, 29.5% ≤ $100k
- Small vendor GSA MAS share in software SINs only 4-5%
- High small vendor share in services SINs (85% in 54151S) reflects services labor

**Entry Strategy:**
- **Not a software product market** — software purchases are large enterprise licenses
- Services subcontracting available but not software product sales

### 5. D304 — NOT A SOFTWARE MARKET 🚫 DEPRIORITIZE

**Strengths:**
- 34.0% ≤ $40k fraction (decent)

**Weaknesses:**

- Small vendor high shares inflated by tiny software market slice
- SEWP negligible ($1.8M total)

**Entry Strategy:**

## Limitations

1. USAspending API 500-page limit caps award fetches at 5,000 per query
2. Time-window comparison uses same 5,000-award sample for both periods (no temporal separation in this run)
3. "Direct/Unknown" vehicle classification conflates true direct awards with unclassified vehicles
4. Small vendor definition (single-award recipients) may not capture all small businesses
5. Description-based SIN/category inference is heuristic; actual holdings require GSA eLibrary/SEWP catalog cross-reference
6. DA01/D304 are not software markets (per E203); their high service SIN shares reflect services labor
