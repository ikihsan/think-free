## What this gate does and does not establish

It establishes that the instrument separates a question answerable from general
knowledge from one needing data the assistant cannot reach. That is the
discrimination F096's claim needs, and it is the discrimination the committed
`probes.jsonl` could not test — those labels were "did the thread resolve",
which is the quantity under test.

It does **not** establish that the instrument is a fair proxy for *a free
general assistant as a user meets one*. The instrument is a frontier model, and
a frontier model is a stronger assistant than the median free one. A served
share measured here is an **upper bound** on what an ordinary user would
experience. This ceiling is stated here rather than discovered afterwards.
