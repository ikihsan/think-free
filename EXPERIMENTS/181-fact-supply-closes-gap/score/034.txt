PROPOSED ANSWER:
SLNX no longer sits behind a preview flag you have to enable - you need the dotnet SDK 9.0.200 or higher and then it just works, so the two posts you found are not contradictory. What is still true is the caution on the page they link to: the format is designed for wide adoption but they recommend waiting until it is more finalized before fully adopting it, which is why it is still beta in practice. So the honest position is that you can use it on 9.0.200+, and there is no roadmap telling you when it stops being provisional. If you need authoritative behaviour before then, the vs-solutionpersistence repository is the closest thing to documentation, and until it firms up the schema will keep moving.
