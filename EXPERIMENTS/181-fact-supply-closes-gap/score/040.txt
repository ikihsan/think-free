PROPOSED ANSWER:
The other thread's detail does not bear on this. What you are seeing is a system-inserted container: assign both a custom inputAccessoryView and a custom inputView to the same UITextField and UIKit inserts a grey view between them, and in iOS 26 it is rendered with the Liquid Glass material. Your minimal red/blue example is the clean reproduction, and it is not your constraints or your colours. No public API removes that container. Work around it by not using UIToolbar as the accessory - make inputAccessoryView a plain UIView with a clear or systemBackground colour, add your own Done button, size it yourself - and keep the picker as inputView. hidesSharedBackground is not the switch you want; it governs the bar item's own background.
