PROPOSED ANSWER:
The APK/bundle detail is from a different thread and does not apply here. What you have is a UIKit message, not a SwiftUI error: a _UIReparentingView is being added under the hosting controller's view by the new iOS 26 menu presentation path. Since it shows on 26.1 and 26.2 and not on iOS 18, treat it as a regression in that path. Avoid it by replacing Menu with MenuPicker on iOS 17+, or by presenting a plain popover with .popover or a custom sheet, and by removing the ZStack and VStack nesting so one view owns the presentation. Test on a device as well as the simulator. Any updateVisibleMenuWithBlock warning you see afterwards is explicitly harmless and does nothing.

ACCEPTED ANSWER ON THE SITE:
In my case, I was getting the same warning and animation glitch when a `Menu` was inside a `GlassEffectContainer` with `.buttonStyle(.glass)` and . `glassEffectUnion()` . 
 Adding `.compositingGroup()` before the button style fixed it: 
 `Menu {
 // menu content...
} label: {
 Image(systemName: "ellipsis")
}
.compositingGroup() // fixes _UIReparentingView warning
.buttonStyle(.glass)
` 
 The `.compositingGroup()` creates a rendering boundary that isolates the `Menu` from parent effects, preventing the UIKit view hierarchy conflict when the menu presents. 
 Even if you're not using glass effects, `.compositingGroup()` on its own might still resolve your issue since it seems to be a general SwiftUI/Menu bug in iOS 26 related to how `Menu` 's UIKit presentation interacts with the view hierarchy.
