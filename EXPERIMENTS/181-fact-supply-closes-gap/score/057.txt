PROPOSED ANSWER:
The grey strip is inserted by UIKit, not by your toolbar: when a UITextField has both a custom inputAccessoryView and a custom inputView, the system inserts a container between the accessory bar and the input area, and in iOS 26 that container is drawn with the new Liquid Glass material. Your simplified red/blue example demonstrates the trigger cleanly. There is no public API to remove that container, and hidesSharedBackground does not - it affects the bar item's shared background, not the container. The practical fix is to stop using UIToolbar as the accessory: make inputAccessoryView a plain UIView with backgroundColor set to .clear or .systemBackground, add your own Done UIButton inside it, and size it yourself. Keep the UIPickerView as inputView.
