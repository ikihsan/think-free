PROPOSED ANSWER:
This one is settled, just not in writing anywhere. SLNX used to be a preview feature you had to enable in Visual Studio; it no longer is, and all you need is the dotnet SDK 9.0.200 or higher - which is not itself a preview SDK. What has not changed is Microsoft's own recommendation on the page they link to: the .SLNX format is designed for wide adoption but they suggest waiting until it is more finalized before adopting it fully, which is why it still reads as a beta. So the documentation gap you are complaining about is real and the best available substitutes are the vs-solutionpersistence repository, whose code you can read as documentation, and trial and error. No roadmap for finalization appears to exist.
