PROPOSED ANSWER:
That single line is the whole diagnosis: your resolved Metro config has unstable_enablePackageExports set to false, so Firebase 11's subpath entry points are being resolved through the legacy fields instead of the exports map, and the failure surfaces as a confusing registration error. Fix it by importing the entry points that work under that resolution - pull app and auth from '@firebase/app' rather than 'firebase/app', and audit every other firebase subpath the same way. Or turn exports on: in metro.config.js, getDefaultConfig(__dirname), then resolver.sourceExts.push('cjs') and set resolver.unstable_enablePackageExports, and export that config. Delete node_modules and restart after any metro.config change. Neither file renames nor cache clears help, because the resolver never reaches your code.
