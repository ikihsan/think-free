Those BuildHost-net472 and BuildHost-netcore folders are generated by the build, not authored by you: in current tooling they are transient host projects spun up for legacy designers and design-time builds, referenced under the solution directory, and recreated on demand. So nothing in your solution layout is at fault and deleting the references by hand will not stick. Treat them as intermediate output - keep them out of source control and exclude the paths if your template globs the solution directory. If you want them gone from the IDE's solution explorer, exclude them in the project file rather than editing the .sln.
