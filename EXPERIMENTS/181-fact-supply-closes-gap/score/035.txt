PROPOSED ANSWER:
The other thread's version detail does not apply, so set it aside; nothing in what you added changes the diagnosis. Your .so files are already 16 KB aligned, so what remains is the APK-level warning, which comes from how the APK itself was packaged. zipalign defaults to 4 KB pages, and the fix is to run it with -P 16 instead of 4 and repackage, then verify with zipalign -c -P 16 and check_elf_alignment.sh on the extracted libraries. Also make sure you are not looking at a stale output, and check each build variant, because the flag often shows up only in release-ish ones. Once the apk is aligned with 16 the warning clears; it is not a further library problem.
