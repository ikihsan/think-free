PROPOSED ANSWER:
The warning you are still seeing is not about the native libraries any more - you have already established they are 16 KB aligned. It is an APK-level warning, and the usual cause is that the APK you are analysing was packaged with zipalign's default page size of 4 rather than 16. Check the artefact you built: re-run zipalign with -P 16 instead of 4 and repackage, then confirm with zipalign -c -P 16 and, on the extracted libraries, with check_elf_alignment.sh. Also make sure you are not looking at a stale output from a different build variant - the warning often appears only in release-ish variants. Once zipalign is run with 16 the APK-level flag clears; it is not a further library problem.
