PROPOSED ANSWER:
Those two facts together say the format is usable but not finished. SLNX no longer sits behind a preview flag you must enable - it needs the dotnet SDK 9.0.200 or higher and then works - so the two Microsoft posts are not contradictory in the way you read them. What is still true is the recommendation on the page they link to: the format is designed for wide adoption but they ask you to wait until it is more finalized before adopting it fully, which is why it is still beta in practice. So you can use it on 9.0.200+, and there is no roadmap for when it stops being provisional. Until the schema firms up, the vs-solutionpersistence repository is the closest thing to documentation, and it will keep moving.
