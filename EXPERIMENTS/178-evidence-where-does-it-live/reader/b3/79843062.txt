=== TITLE ===
.NET 10 version update generates BuildHost-net472 and BuildHost-netcore folder shortcuts

=== QUESTION ===
After updating a .NET Core app version from .NET 8 to .NET 10, BuildHost-net472 and BuildH
ost-netcore folder shortcuts appeared in both projects in the solution, then deleted and w
orks well, they also appeared in published project files. Are those files important?

=== KEY ANSWER (highest scored) ===
These folders are projected from the Nuget package (EntityFrameworkCore.Design). 
 Seems like they are now needed for dotnet-ef operations (e.g. migrations). The folders ar
e also copied to the publishing output, what I would consider a bug (they are not needed a
t runtime). 
 At least you could hide them from the solution/project view by adding this section to you
r .csproj file(s): 
 <ItemGroup>
 <Content Update="@(Content)">
 <Visible Condition="'%(NuGetItemType)' == 'Content'">false</Visible>
 </Content>
</ItemGroup>
 

 <ItemGroup>
 <Content Update="@(Content)">
 <Visible Condition="'%(NuGetItemType)' == 'Content'">false</Visible>
 </Content>
</ItemGroup>
