 What is Domain Rating (DR)? 
 
 Think of Domain Rating (DR) as a website's internet popularity score. Measured on a simple scale from 0 to 100, this metric shows the overall strength of a website based on how many other places on the web link back to it. 
 The calculation doesn't look at traffic or content quality. Instead, it relies on digital recommendations. It basically figures out: 
 
 How many unique websites are currently pointing to yours. 
 The overall trustworthiness and authority score of those websites. 
 How many other sites those referring pages are recommending at the same time. 
 
 A higher score means a website has a strong network of digital nods, which usually helps it stand out and look much more credible to major search engines. It's a perfect metric for finding new partnership opportunities, peeking at what your competitors are doing right, and tracking your own growth over time. 
 
 

 
 
 Frequently Asked Questions 
 
 
 
 
 Is this online checker free to use?
 
 
 
 Yes, it is entirely free! You can check batches of up to 50 websites at a time as often as you want without creating an account, signing up for subscriptions, or entering payment details. 
 
 

 
 
 Why is there a limit of 50 websites per check?
 
 
 
 We keep the limit at 50 per search so that the tool stays incredibly fast and free for everyone. If you have a longer list of websites to check, just break them down into a few small batches and process them one after another. 
 
 

 
 
 What score is considered good or high?
 
 
 
 Scores are relative. Generally speaking, a rating between 30 and 50 is quite common for average businesses, 50 to 70 means a site is strong and popular, and anything over 70 is outstanding. However, a "good" score simply means matching or beating your direct business competitors. 
 
 

 
 
 Does a high score guarantee higher search rankings?
 
 
 
 Not always, but it helps significantly. Search engines look at hundreds of indicators to rank content, including helpful writing, fast page loading, and user experience. A high popularity score just means your underlying digital link network is very strong, which gives your site a competitive edge. 
 
 

 
 
 How often do these authority numbers change?
 
 
 
 The metrics are updated continuously as web crawlers discover new recommendations and remove broken ones across the internet. You might see small shifts every few weeks depending on how active other websites are in your industry. 
 
 

 
 
 Can I raise my website's score manually?
 
 
 
 The most effective way to grow your rating is by naturally earning links from popular, trusted websites in your market space. Creating high-quality articles, sharing helpful resources, and partnering with industry publishers will make other sites want to link to you.
