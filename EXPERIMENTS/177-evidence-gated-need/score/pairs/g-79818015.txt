The extra translucent strip is added by the system, not by your toolbar: in iOS 26 the inp
ut container between inputView and inputAccessoryView is drawn with the new Liquid Glass m
aterial, and it appears when both a custom inputView and a custom inputAccessoryView are s
et. There is no public API to remove that container background. The practical fix is to st
op using UIToolbar as the accessory: build a plain UIView containing a Done UIButton, set 
its backgroundColor to .clear or .systemBackground as needed, and assign that as inputAcce
ssoryView. Keep the button's configuration colors explicit rather than relying on tint inh
eritance, and size the view yourself.
