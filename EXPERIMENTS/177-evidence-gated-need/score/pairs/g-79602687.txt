=== LEFT ===
That message means the native side asked AppRegistry for a component literally named auth,
 which matches the app's registered name rather than anything you pass to registerComponen
t. So your index.js is either missing registerRootComponent(App), or it does not import th
e App you think it does, or app.json/package.json name is auth while nothing registers. Ch
eck that index.js does import { registerRootComponent } from 'expo', imports App from './A
pp', and calls registerRootComponent(App), that main points at index.js, and that expo.nam
e/slug in app.json match your project. Renaming files and clearing Metro caches can never 
fix a name or registration mismatch; also confirm nobody registers a component called auth
.
