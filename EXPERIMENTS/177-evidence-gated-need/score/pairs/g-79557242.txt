=== LEFT ===
R's vectors concatenate, so you need to interleave explicitly. The shortest answer is as.v
ector(rbind(vec, paste0(vec, '.1'))): rbind makes a two-row matrix and as.vector reads it 
column-major, giving x, x.1, y, y.1, z, z.1. Equally explicit: paste0(rep(vec, each = 2), 
rep(c('', '.1'), times = length(vec))). A base-R loop works too: unlist(lapply(vec, functi
on(x) c(x, paste0(x, '.1'))), use.names = FALSE). The rep(each = 2) plus a repeated suffix
 vector is usually clearest if the suffix may later vary per element.

=== RIGHT ===
You can use make.unique + rep 
 make.unique(rep(vec, each = 2))
[1] "x" "x.1" "y" "y.1" "z" "z.1"
