=== LEFT ===
Your reading is right: declaring an implicit intent filter with ACTION_MAIN (and, in pract
ice, CATEGORY_LAUNCHER) makes almost every installed app visible to you under Android 11+ 
package visibility filtering. It is not QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: no permission is involved, and
 you see only what the query returns. Google Play policy still applies: broad queries must
 be justified as core functionality (a launcher, a default-handler or share-target chooser
, automation, backup), and Play scrutinises MAIN/LAUNCHER declarations; using the queries 
mechanism to enumerate apps without a legitimate core feature is a policy violation even w
ithout the permission. Declare specific packages with a package element or a narrower inte
nt with a specific action, category or data scheme, and keep the Play Console declaration 
in sync.
