=== LEFT ===
Your own stylesheet is loaded after Tailwind's, and some of your selectors are more specif
ic than the utilities, so you are overriding them. nav.container (class plus element) beat
s p-6 (single class), .navbar a beats space-x-6's margin utility, and .btn-nav with paddin
g-block/padding-inline overrides py-2/px-6 because your sheet comes last. The universal re
set * {margin:0;padding:0} is not needed, since Tailwind v4 already ships preflight. Remov
e the hand-written rules, or move them into Tailwind's cascade with @layer components/base
 inside your CSS entry so utilities win, or express them as @utility so they participate i
n variants and ordering.
