re is active opposition in the committee, so nobody can promise C++26 or C++29 content. Ex
pect incremental opt-in work: annotations, constexpr-friendly checks, better static diagno
stics and library-level safe abstractions, none of which give dynamic memory safety. Track
 the WG21 paper list and P3081's status pages for real movement, and treat blog or LLM cla
ims of safe C++ in C++26 as unverified.
