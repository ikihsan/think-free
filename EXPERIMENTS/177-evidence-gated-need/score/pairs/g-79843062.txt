These are transient intermediate artifacts created by MSBuild's BuildHost targets, which s
pin up small net472/netcore host projects for legacy designers and design-time builds (WPF
/WinForms designer or the Visual Studio design-time build). They are regenerated on demand
 and are safe to delete; nothing at runtime or publish depends on them. They normally live
 under obj, so if they appear at the project root a tool or an older build configuration p
ut them there: delete them and keep obj ignored. If they end up inside your publish output
, check that your publish item globs are not picking up the project root, and clean obj an
d bin before republishing. Checking git status for the exact paths will confirm where they
 came from.
