That is a deprecation warning from the AGP DSL inspection in Android Studio, not an error;
 your project still builds and produces the same manifest. AGP has been renaming these acc
essors over the years (compileSdkVersion to compileSdk in AGP 7), and newer AGP versions d
eprecate older spellings in favour of the newer property surface, so a strikethrough on th
e Groovy accessor in 8.12 is that deprecation, not a reversal. Hover the word to read the 
exact replacement AGP suggests and check the AGP 8.12 release notes for the DSL migration;
 if you want to stay put, keep whichever spelling AGP now prefers or suppress the inspecti
on. Semantically nothing changes: compileSdk selects the API you compile against, targetSd
k is the behaviour level you opt into.
