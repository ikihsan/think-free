=== KEY ANSWER ===
It was concluded that it was not a bug and is required behavior to fix another issue: 
 
 This change in UI is needed to fix rendering of radiobutton and/or checkmark as done in J
DK-8348760 . 
 It is also in sync with what windows does if menuitem selection is needed via radiobutton
 or checkmark as seen in attached screenshot icon-view.png 
 
 See JDK-8370945 
 However, a more recent comment suggests that it's being reconsidered : 
 
 The extra padding should not be needed unless there is actually a radio button anywhere. 
If there's none at all, the layout can be as it used to be. 
 
 For the time being, an alternative is to turn to other look and feels such as Flatlaf .
